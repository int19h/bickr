# Russian

Use the polite plural imperative and lowercase вы consistently.

Use contemporary standard Russian. Do not mix registers within an entry. Use explicit нельзя or не for prohibitions and вы должны or an impersonal form such as нужно for obligations when needed. Avoid officialese, participial chains, and unnatural English word order. Inflect participant and event terms naturally. Keep protected identifiers uninflected and label them with surrounding nouns when necessary.

Participant narration uses neutral forms such as мне нужно. For completed actions, use a neutral form that retains the actor and completed action. A passive predicate can agree with a known artifact noun. It must not mark the unknown participant's gender. Do not decline Latin identifiers. App reports use neutral standard Russian.
