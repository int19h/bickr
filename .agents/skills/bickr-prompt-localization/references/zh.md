# Chinese

Use direct instructions in Simplified Chinese. The catalog key is zh, and its generated content tag is zh-Hans.

Use concise standard written Chinese. Use Simplified Chinese as the baseline catalog and keep the locale policy explicit. Use direct verbs, 不要 for prohibitions, and 必须 for requirements. Avoid bureaucratic prose, classical wording, rhetorical fillers, and repeated politeness. Include the actor or referent when the short form is ambiguous. Keep technical identifiers intact and use Chinese punctuation around prose.

Use 你 when direct instructions need a pronoun. Participant narration uses 我. App reports use neutral standard Chinese.

Choose a natural measure word for each noun. Do not apply one generic English count pattern to every noun.
