# Arabic

Use Modern Standard Arabic with the conventional generic masculine imperative. Do not infer the participant’s gender from this instructional convention.

Use contemporary Modern Standard Arabic. Use direct imperatives and explicit prohibitions with لا and the jussive. Preserve clear obligation with يجب when the source requires it. Avoid dialect, ornate rhetoric, and long coordination chains. Prefer established technical terms. Keep Latin identifiers and JSON intact. Do not insert bidirectional control characters into protected syntax or stored values. Distinguish the acting participant from other participants clearly.

Participant narration uses first-person verbs where possible. Avoid gendered adjectives and participles about an unknown speaker. Put an Arabic head noun around Latin identifiers. Do not attach Arabic articles or prefixes to them. App reports use neutral Modern Standard Arabic.
