# Belarusian

Use the polite plural imperative and lowercase вы consistently.

Use contemporary standard Belarusian consistently. Preserve obligation with трэба or вы павінны and prohibition with не or нельга. Avoid Russian calques and archaizing vocabulary. Use ordinary Belarusian words, natural inflection, and short clauses. Keep identifiers intact.

Use the official 2008 orthography (narkamaŭka) consistently. Participant narration uses neutral forms such as мне трэба. Preserve completed actions and the actor through neutral formulations. A past passive can agree with a known artifact noun. It cannot assign gender to the participant. Do not replace a completed action with a planned action or a general ability. A gender-neutral resultative or impersonal form can preserve completion. A finite past verb that marks the speaker's gender is not neutral. App reports use neutral standard Belarusian.

A passive predicate can agree with a known artifact noun. It must not mark the unknown participant's gender. Keep the original actor and completed action explicit when needed.

Use explicit catalog patterns for lists and relative time. In the current Workers runtime, Intl.ListFormat and Intl.RelativeTimeFormat fall back to English for Belarusian. Intl.PluralRules supports Belarusian. Bickr uses explicit Belarusian catalog patterns and rejects missing entries.

Use павінен or трэба as a consistent style choice for obligations. The word мусіць also exists in standard Belarusian. It does not identify an orthography by itself. Use сродак кіравання for a Bickr control and інструмент for a tool.
