# Esperanto

Use the imperative for instructions and vi when the addressee needs a pronoun.

Use standard Esperanto with transparent roots and ordinary syntax. Use imperatives for actions, ne for prohibitions, and devas for requirements. Use povas for ability or permission, as the context requires. Do not strengthen permission into devas. Avoid rare neologisms, ornate derivations, and excessive compound words. Follow agreement and accusative rules. Introduce technical terms consistently and leave protected identifiers intact.

Participant narration uses mi. App reports use neutral standard Esperanto. Put case endings on a head noun, never on an identifier. Esperanto needs explicit catalog patterns for lists and relative time. In the current Workers runtime, Intl.ListFormat and Intl.RelativeTimeFormat fall back to English for Esperanto. Intl.PluralRules supports Esperanto. Bickr uses explicit Esperanto catalog patterns and rejects missing entries.

Use singular nouns in one and plural nouns in other. Adjectives agree with their nouns.
