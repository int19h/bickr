# Italian

Use the singular imperative consistently.

Use neutral standard Italian. Preserve requirements with devi, bisogna or è necessario and prohibitions with non. Prefer short clauses and ordinary technical vocabulary. Avoid bureaucratic nominalizations, ornamental expressions, and regional speech. Keep actors and referents explicit where omitted subjects can confuse them.

Use the tu imperative and non plus the infinitive for prohibitions. Participant narration uses neutral first-person predicates. Avoid essere plus a gendered participle about an unknown speaker. App reports use neutral standard Italian.

The many category includes large round counts. Count parameters render as ASCII digits. A category does not insert the words milione or milioni. Use di before a noun when the written numeral construction requires it. A many variant can match other when the numeral consists entirely of digits.
