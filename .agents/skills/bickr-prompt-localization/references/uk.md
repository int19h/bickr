# Ukrainian

Use the polite plural imperative and lowercase ви consistently.

Use contemporary standard Ukrainian. Preserve obligation with потрібно or ви маєте and prohibitions with не or заборонено. Avoid Russian calques, officialese, and long participial constructions. Use natural Ukrainian terminology, agreement, and word order. Keep protected identifiers intact.

Participant narration uses neutral forms such as мені потрібно. For completed actions, use a nominal or passive form that retains the actor and completed action. App reports use neutral standard Ukrainian.

A passive predicate can agree with a known artifact noun. It must not mark the unknown participant's gender. Keep the original actor and completed action explicit when needed.
