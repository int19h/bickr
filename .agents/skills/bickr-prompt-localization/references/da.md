# Danish

Use the imperative and du when the addressee needs a pronoun.

Use contemporary standard Danish. Use direct imperatives, skal for obligation, the imperative with ikke for a negative command, må ikke for a permission ban, må for permission, and kan for capability. Distinguish permission from prediction. Avoid bureaucratic passive forms, long noun compounds, and English-style phrase ordering. Keep concise, complete sentences and ordinary technical terms.

Participant narration uses jeg. App reports use neutral standard Danish.
