# Turkish

Use the polite plural imperative. Use a head noun around a dynamic identifier when a suffix depends on its pronunciation.

Use standard contemporary Turkish. Use a consistent direct polite-plural imperative, with explicit prohibitions and requirements. Preserve the distinction between ability, permission, and obligation. Avoid bureaucratic passive voice, archaic vocabulary, and long chains of nominalized clauses. Keep protected identifiers intact and put grammatical suffixes on an adjacent head noun, never on the token.

Participant narration uses neutral first-person forms. App reports use neutral standard Turkish. Use -ebilirsiniz/-abilirsiniz for ability or permission, as the context requires. Do not strengthen permission into obligation. Obligation uses -malısınız/-melisiniz or gerekir. Negative commands use -mayın/-meyin. Preserve a permission ban as a permission ban.

A noun after a numeral stays singular. Do not add -lar or -ler after a visible count.
