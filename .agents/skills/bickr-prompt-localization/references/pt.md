# Portuguese

Use the third-person imperative, such as leia, escreva, and não escreva, with the personal pronoun omitted. Use broad vocabulary and the 1990 orthographic agreement consistently.

Use neutral contemporary Portuguese with broadly understandable vocabulary. Use a consistent direct instructional register. Prefer imperatives without excessive personal address. Preserve obligation with deve or é obrigatório and prohibition with não. Avoid gerund chains, officialese, regional slang, and gratuitous diminutives. Preserve natural agreement and explicit subject references when needed.

Use seu or sua where a possessive is needed. Agreement follows the possessed noun, not the addressee's gender. Broad vocabulary means words understood across varieties. Participant narration uses neutral first-person predicates without gendered adjectives. App reports use neutral standard Portuguese.

The many category includes large round counts. Count parameters render as ASCII digits. A category does not insert the words milhão or milhões. Use de before a noun when the written numeral construction requires it. A many variant can match other when the numeral consists entirely of digits.
