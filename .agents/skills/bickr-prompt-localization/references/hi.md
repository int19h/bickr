# Hindi

Use the neutral respectful imperative and आप when the addressee needs a pronoun.

Use contemporary standard Hindi in Devanagari with familiar technical terms. Use a consistent neutral respectful imperative, not ceremonial honorifics. Preserve obligation with आवश्यक है for present requirements and prohibition with न करें. Avoid heavily Sanskritized replacements for familiar terms, regional slang, and long English noun chains. Name the actor when omission is ambiguous. Keep Latin identifiers intact.

Use the respectful subjunctive imperative, such as लिखें and न लिखें. For narration, prefer मुझे ... है and completed-action constructions that do not mark the speaker’s gender. Use चाहिए only when its force matches the source. Do not use it to soften a requirement or prohibition. App reports use neutral standard Hindi. Do not change a present requirement into a future prediction.

The one category includes 0. Its sentence must also be grammatical when the visible count is 0.
